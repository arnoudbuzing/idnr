Featured Destination

Kickapoo State Recreation Area

Explore forested hills, 22 deep-water ponds, and the scenic Middle Fork of the Vermilion River in East Central Illinois.

Open Today Vermilion County • Oakwood, IL
East Central Illinois • Off I-74

Middle Fork Scenic River & 22 Clear Ponds

One of the first state parks in the U.S. established on reclaimed surface-mined coal land, Kickapoo now spans forested hills, deep clear ponds for fishing and SCUBA diving, and the federally designated Middle Fork of the Vermilion River.
